# Recorded instrument samples

Guitar acoustic and electric bass recordings from **tonejs-instruments**, curated by N. P. Brosowsky:
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

Samples licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The subset is redistributed unmodified. During playback GTab transposes, envelopes, filters, and reverberates the samples. The upstream license is included in LICENSE.md.
